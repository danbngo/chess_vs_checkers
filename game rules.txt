 CAMPAIGN 1: VS CHECKERS
═══════════════════════════════════

basic rules:

WAVES:
- game is played in waves
- take all the enemy pieces to win a wave
- be careful! pieces you lose are not returned at the end of the wave (unless they have the Iron trait)
- if you win the last wave, you win the game!

MONEY:
- the faster you win, the more money you earn
- you always earn at least $1 after winning a round
- money is used in shops between waves to buy more pieces
- some pieces have special traits - look out for these!

PAWN PROMOTION:
- when a pawn reaches the back rank (row 8) it can promote to a queen or knight
- promoted pieces revert back to pawns at the end of each wave

KINGS & CHECK:
- if you have exactly 1 king, you can be checkmated (instantly lose) and cannot move into check
- if you have 2 or more kings, check rules are suspended - kings move freely and losing one is not fatal
- your king flashes red when it is in check
- you can buy extra kings in the shop (available from wave 3)

EN PASSANT:
- if an enemy checker moves two squares forward to land beside one of your pawns on the 5th rank, your pawn can capture it diagonally on the following move

CHECKER AI:
- checkers must capture if a capture is available (mandatory capture rule)
- after capturing, if further captures are available the checker must keep jumping (multi-jump)
- checkers that reach your back rank promote to checker kings

LIGHT-SQUARE CHECKERS (from wave 2 onwards):
- half the checker army spawns on light squares (shown in red-orange) alongside the standard dark-square checkers (shown in black)
- a checker's tile color is permanent — light checkers always stay on light squares and dark checkers on dark squares
- movement and capture logic works identically on both colors; the diagonals naturally preserve tile color

CHECKER KINGS:
- checker kings move backwards as well as forwards
- from wave 5 onwards, some checker kings are Flying Kings: they can slide any number of squares diagonally (like a bishop) and capture from distance
- not all kings in the enemy army will be flying kings — expect a mix
- when a checker promotes mid-wave, it becomes a flying king if any flying king is still alive in the enemy army; otherwise it becomes a normal king

TRIPLE KINGS:
- a checker king that returns to row 1 (its starting side of the board) promotes to a Triple King (shown with a gold outline)
- triple kings can jump over two consecutive enemy pieces in a single move, capturing both
- triple kings can also hop over allied checker pieces (friendly hop): the allied piece is not captured and this is never mandatory
- triple kings cannot mix an enemy capture and a friendly hop in the same jump
- movement is otherwise identical to regular kings

TRAITS (appear more often in later waves, never in the first shop — scales linearly to 100% by the last shop):
- Mercenary (yellow outline): costs half price, but has a 1/3 chance to desert the army after each capture. Kings cannot be mercenaries.
- Iron (blue outline): costs double, but when captured is NOT permanently lost - the piece returns at the start of the next wave
- Raider (orange outline): costs 1.5x, earns $1 every time it captures an enemy

VARIANT PIECES (appear from wave halfway onwards, reaching normal shop frequency by the final shop):
- Amazon ($110): moves like a queen AND a knight combined. Purple tint.
- Archbishop ($55): moves like a bishop AND a knight combined. Controls both colors. Green tint.
- Chancellor ($70): moves like a rook AND a knight combined. Dominates open files. Cyan tint.
- Grasshopper ($22): slides like a queen but must jump over exactly one piece and land directly behind it. Captures if an enemy is on the landing square. Yellow-green tint.
- Berolina Pawn ($10): mirror of the standard pawn — moves diagonally forward, captures straight ahead. Can move two squares diagonally from its starting rank. Orange tint.
- Camel ($18): leaps in a 3+1 L-shape (like a knight but 3 squares in one direction, 1 perpendicular). Always lands on the opposite tile color. Brown tint.
- Nightrider ($38): repeatedly leaps in the same knight direction until blocked. Captures the first enemy it reaches and stops. Deep purple tint.

 CAMPAIGN 2: VS GO
═══════════════════════════════════

OBJECTIVE:
- capture all go stones on the board to win each wave
- chess pieces land on a go stone to capture it (standard chess capture)

GO STONE PLACEMENT:
- after each of your moves, the go AI places one new stone on an empty square
- go stones never move — they accumulate across your turn

ENCLOSURE (the main threat):
- if go stones completely wall off a region (no orthogonal path to the board edge without crossing a go stone), every chess piece inside that region is instantly removed
- if your king is in an enclosed region when it is sealed, you lose the game immediately
- check and checkmate rules do NOT apply in the go campaign — only enclosure threatens your king

MOVEMENT RESTRICTION:
- chess pieces cannot voluntarily move into an already-enclosed region
- squares blocked by enclosure are highlighted in red when you select a piece (same as illegal moves)
- capturing a go stone is always allowed even if it is on the boundary of an enclosed region

DIFFICULTY:
- each wave starts with more go stones already placed (5 on wave 1, increasing by 3 per wave)
- the go AI prefers to extend existing stone formations to build walls

NOTES:
- go stones cannot be "counter-surrounded" by chess pieces — only chess pieces die to enclosure
- traits, shops, and variant pieces work exactly the same as in the checkers campaign
